Let's Meet

Skillarium Academy of Professional Studies is a beacon of hope for students seeking to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and professional readiness. Born out of a collective experience of facing rejection in the job market due to a lack of practical skills, Skillarium is dedicated to empowering students with the technical expertise needed to thrive in today's competitive landscape.

Our Academics

Our mission extends beyond traditional education; we aim to cultivate well-rounded individuals who can not only excel in their chosen fields but also contribute positively to society. Skillarium offers a comprehensive curriculum encompassing a wide range of computer programming languages, including HTML, CSS, Node.js, Python, Angular, .NET, C, C++, Adobe Creative studio, Arts, as well as technical engineering and management studies.

Our Values

Unlike many institutions that prioritize financial gains, Skillarium is rooted in the compassionate spirit of Kerala. Our commitment to providing quality education is driven by a genuine desire to uplift students and contribute to their overall well-being. We believe that by investing in the future of our youth, we are investing in the progress of our nation.

Guided by a vision of empowering the community and presenting highly skilled graduates to the global market, Skillarium is nurtured by a group of well-wishers who have successfully navigated the corporate world and academic pursuits. We believe that by sharing our experiences and insights, we can equip students with the tools they need to achieve their career aspirations.
