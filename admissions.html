Admissions

Join Skillarium Academy and take the first step towards a successful professional career. Our admissions process is designed to identify passionate learners and provide them with the resources they need to excel.

How to Apply

Applications are accepted year-round. We offer rolling admissions for most programs, with intake periods in January, May, and September. Prospective students can apply online through our application portal or visit our campus in Thrissur for an in-person consultation.

Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Academic transcripts from previous institutions
  • Statement of purpose
  • Letters of recommendation (for select programs)
  • Entrance assessment or interview

Scholarships & Financial Aid

Skillarium is committed to making quality education accessible. We offer merit-based scholarships and need-based financial aid to deserving students. Contact our admissions office to learn more about available opportunities.
