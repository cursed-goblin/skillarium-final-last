Nutrition in Human Beings

Apr 15, 2025 · 5 min read

Nutrition is the science that interprets the nutrients and other substances in food in relation to maintenance, growth, reproduction, health, and disease of an organism. It includes food intake, absorption, assimilation, biosynthesis, catabolism, and excretion.

A balanced diet is essential for maintaining good health. It should include carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, and water in the right proportions. Each nutrient plays a specific role in the body.

Carbohydrates are the body's primary energy source. Proteins are essential for growth and repair of tissues. Fats provide energy and support cell growth. Vitamins and minerals are needed in small amounts but are crucial for various bodily functions.

Malnutrition, whether undernutrition or overnutrition, can lead to serious health problems. Understanding nutritional needs is fundamental to maintaining a healthy lifestyle and preventing disease.
